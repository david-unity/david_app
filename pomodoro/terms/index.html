2. What the app does

Pomodoro is a Pomodoro timer. It runs focus sessions with short breaks and a long break after a set number of focus sessions, can start the next session automatically, lets you organise sessions into categories with colours, tracks a daily goal, shows statistics, plays built-in background sounds, and shows your timer on your Home Screen and Lock Screen, in a Live Activity, and in the Mac menu bar.
