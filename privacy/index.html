Data we collect

We may collect the following categories of information, depending on app features you use:

  • Personal information: email address, display name, or contact details you provide when contacting support.
  • Usage & analytics: anonymous usage data (crash reports, diagnostics, feature usage) to improve stability and experience.
  • Device & technical data: device model, OS version, app version, and unique device identifiers for debugging and analytics.

Device permissions

The app only requests permissions that are necessary for features you choose to use. Examples include:

  • Camera / Photos: requested only for optional features that let you take or select images. Images remain on your device unless you explicitly upload or share them.
  • Microphone: requested only if a feature requires voice input; recordings are not collected without your consent.
  • Location: requested only for location-specific features and only while using the feature (if applicable).
  • Notifications: used to deliver opt-in alerts; you may disable them anytime in iOS Settings.

If you deny a permission the related functionality will be disabled, and you can change permissions in iOS Settings for the app.
