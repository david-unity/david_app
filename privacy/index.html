Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026 — Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. Our commitment to your privacy

We take your privacy and the security of your data seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what data the David App collects, why we collect it, how it is used, and the choices you have. We design the app to collect as little data as possible, and we never sell your personal information.

2. Data we collect

We may collect the following categories of information, depending on the app features you use:

  • Personal information: email address, display name, or contact details you provide when contacting support.
  • Usage & analytics: anonymous usage data (crash reports, diagnostics, feature usage) to improve stability and experience.
  • Device & technical data: device model, OS version, app version, and unique device identifiers for debugging and analytics.

3. Device permissions

The app only requests permissions that are necessary for features you choose to use. Examples include:

  • Camera / Photos: requested only for optional features that let you take or select images. Images remain on your device unless you explicitly upload or share them.
  • Microphone: requested only if a feature requires voice input; recordings are not collected without your consent.
  • Location: requested only for location-specific features and only while using the feature (if applicable).
  • Notifications: used to deliver opt-in alerts; you may disable them anytime in iOS Settings.

If you deny a permission the related functionality will be disabled, and you can change permissions at any time in iOS Settings for the app.

4. Third-party services and sharing

We may share limited data with trusted third parties to provide services, such as analytics and crash reporting. These providers process data according to their own policies and only to the extent necessary to perform those services on our behalf.

  • Crash reporting and analytics providers (to help diagnose issues).
  • Hosting providers for server-side features (when applicable).

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers. Any sharing is minimal, controlled, and necessary for the services above.

5. Purchases and advertising data

If you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store; we do not receive or store your payment details. We may keep minimal purchase-related data (such as a receipt token and purchase timestamp) solely to verify and restore your entitlement to paid features.

If the app displays third-party advertisements, ad networks may collect device and usage information (such as advertising identifiers) to deliver and measure ads. You control ad personalization through your device settings and App Tracking Transparency.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the app and comply with our legal obligations. If you would like your data deleted, please contact us (see Contact information). Upon request we will delete or anonymize your data, unless we are required to retain it by law.

7. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data, including encryption for data in transit (TLS) and access controls. While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The David App is not intended for children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps to remove that information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy here with a revised effective date. Significant changes will be communicated where feasible.
