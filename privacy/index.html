Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

Our commitment to your privacy

We prioritize your privacy and data security. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control your information. We minimize data collection and secure what is necessary to provide and improve the app.

Data we collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Personal info: email or contact details you provide for support.
  • Usage & analytics: crash reports, diagnostics, feature usage (generally anonymized).
  • Device data: OS version, device model, app version, and identifiers for diagnostics.

Device permissions

The app requests only permissions required by optional features (camera, microphone, location, notifications). You can disable permissions in iOS Settings; disabling will only affect the related feature.

Advertising, paywall & in-app purchases

The app may show third-party ads and offers premium features through a paywall (in-app purchases):

  • Ads: Third-party ad networks may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads. They follow their own privacy policies.
  • IAP / Paywall: Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store). We may store minimal purchase data (receipt token, timestamp) to verify and restore purchases.
  • Controls: Use iOS App Tracking Transparency and ad settings to control personalized ads.

Third-party services & sharing

We share limited data with trusted providers for analytics, crash reporting, ads, and purchase verification. These providers process data according to their own policies. We do not sell personal data.

Data retention & deletion

We retain data only as needed to operate the app and comply with legal obligations. To request deletion or access, contact us (see below) and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Security

We apply standard safeguards (TLS in transit, access controls) to protect data. No system is perfectly secure—report suspected issues to us immediately.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. We will post any revisions here with an updated effective date.
