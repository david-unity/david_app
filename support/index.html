Before you write: how to get a faster fix

Including the details below in your first email usually saves several rounds of back-and-forth:

  1. Your device model (for example, iPhone 14 Pro) and iOS version.
  2. The app version you are using (shown in the app's Settings or About screen).
  3. A short description of what you expected and what actually happened.
  4. The steps to reproduce the issue, in order.
  5. A screenshot or screen recording, if the issue is visual.

Please never include passwords, payment card numbers, or other sensitive credentials in a support email. We will never ask you for them.

Frequently asked questions

The app crashes or freezes — what should I do?

Please try these steps first, in order:

  • Force-quit the app and reopen it.
  • Restart your device.
  • Update to the latest app version from the App Store.
  • Make sure your iOS version is up to date.
  • As a last resort, delete and reinstall the app (note: locally stored data may be lost).

If the problem persists, email us with your device model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that lead to the crash.

How do I restore a purchase or subscription?

Open the app, go to the purchase or premium screen, and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

If the restore does not work, email us the approximate purchase date and the Apple ID region (not your password), and we will help verify your entitlement.

How do I request a refund or cancel a subscription?

All purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Refunds, cancellations, and billing disputes are handled by Apple, not by us.

  • Refunds: visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.
  • Cancel a subscription: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

If Apple's flow does not resolve your issue, contact us and we will assist where we can.

Why does the app ask for camera, photo, microphone, or location access?

The app only requests permissions needed for features you choose to use, and requests them at the moment the feature is used. If you deny a permission, the related feature is simply disabled — the rest of the app keeps working.

You can change any permission at any time in iOS Settings → David App. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Notifications are opt-in. You can enable or disable them at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → David App.

What data does the app collect?

We collect as little as possible: contact details you provide when emailing support, anonymous usage and crash diagnostics, and basic device/technical data used for debugging. We never sell your personal information.

See the Privacy Policy for the complete breakdown.

How do I request deletion of my data?

Email david.dev.unity@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" from the email address associated with your enquiry.

We will delete or anonymise your data unless we are legally required to retain it, and confirm once it is done. You may also request access to or correction of your data the same way.

I have a feature request or general feedback.

Feedback is genuinely welcome and shapes what gets built next. Email us with the subject "Feature Request" and describe the problem you would like solved — knowing the why behind a request is often more useful than the exact feature.

Do you support children's accounts?

The David App is not intended for children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it promptly.

Report a security issue

If you believe you have found a security or privacy vulnerability in the David App, please report it privately to david.dev.unity@gmail.com rather than disclosing it publicly. Include the steps to reproduce and any relevant details, and we will investigate and respond as quickly as possible.
