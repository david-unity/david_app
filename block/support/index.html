Before you write: how to get a faster fix

Including the details below in your first email helps us understand the problem faster:

  1. Your device model and iOS version.
  2. The app version.
  3. A short description of what you expected and what actually happened.
  4. The steps to reproduce the issue, in order.
  5. A screenshot of the problem.

Please do not include passwords or payment card details in an email.

Frequently asked questions

How do I give Block the Screen Time permission?

Block uses Apple's Screen Time API. iOS asks for Screen Time permission when you create your first group. You can change it later in iOS Settings → Screen Time.

What is included in the free version, and what does Block Pro add?

The free version includes one group with one app or website.

Block Pro adds unlimited groups, unlimited apps and websites per group, and whole app categories.

Block Pro is available as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription (with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers), or a lifetime one-time purchase.

How do I restore purchases?

Tap Restore purchases on the Block Pro screen, or in the app's Settings.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. To manage or cancel, open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store, so refund requests are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.

What are the requirements?

Block App runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
