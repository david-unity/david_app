2. What the app does

You put apps, app categories or websites into a group and choose a block style for it: Hide removes the apps from your Home Screen until you switch the group off, Schedule blocks them during set hours on the days you choose, and Daily quota allows a set number of minutes per day, then blocks them for the rest of the day. The app also shows a 7-day usage chart, offers an optional passcode with Face ID, and can turn off installing apps and in-app purchases on the device.

3. Screen Time and its limits

The app works through Apple's Screen Time technology and needs your permission to use it. What can be blocked, and how, is decided by iOS, not by the app. Please keep in mind:

  • Blocking depends on Screen Time working on your device. iOS updates, device restrictions, or turning off Screen Time access for the app can stop or change blocking.
  • Deleting the app removes every block. This is a limit of iOS, not a fault of the app.
  • The app is a tool to help you focus. We do not promise that every app, category or website will be blocked at every moment, and the app is not a parental-control or security product.
