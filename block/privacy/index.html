Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026 — Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Block App (home-screen name "Block"), an iPhone app that helps you block distracting apps, app categories and websites on your own device ("the app"). The app is published by David App ("we", "us").

This policy explains what the app accesses on your device, what information is collected, why, and the choices you have.

2. Summary

  • The app has no account, no login.
  • The app does not show advertisements and does not track you. No IDFA is used.
  • We do not sell or share your data for advertising.
  • Your selections and rules stay on your device.
  • We use Google Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. This data is not linked to your identity.

3. Screen Time and information that stays on your device

The app uses Apple's Screen Time API (FamilyControls) to block apps, app categories and websites on your own device. It uses individual authorization for your own device; it does not monitor other people, such as children or family members.

  • Screen Time permission: the app asks for Screen Time permission when you create your first group. You can change it at any time in iOS Settings → Screen Time.
  • Your selections: the apps, categories and websites you pick are opaque tokens from Apple. The app cannot see their names.
  • Your rules and groups: selections and rules are stored only on your device, in a container shared with the app's own extensions. They are not sent to any server of ours.
  • Screen Time usage data: shown by Apple's DeviceActivityReport extension on your device. It never leaves the device.
  • Passcode: if you set a passcode, it is stored in the device Keychain.
  • Face ID: if you use Face ID to unlock the app, Face ID is handled by iOS. The app never receives biometric data.

4. Information collected by third-party services

The app uses the following services from Google:

  • Firebase Analytics: anonymous usage statistics, including purchase events such as which plan was bought.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports, used to find and fix crashes.

The data types collected through Firebase are: crash data, other diagnostic data, device ID (Firebase installation ID), product interaction, purchase history (which Block Pro plan was bought), and coarse location (approximate region derived from IP address).

This data is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. Each provider processes data under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy · Firebase privacy information.

5. Advertising and tracking

The app does not display advertisements, does not track you, and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not sell or share data for advertising.

6. In-app purchases

The "Block Pro" in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app never sees your payment details.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
